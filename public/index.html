Underwriting Methodology Formulas

Our calculation engine is built on standard Indian and International Banking Underwriting Guidelines:

1. FOIR Capacity = Net Monthly Income × Tier FOIR Cap (40% - 65%)
2. Max Monthly EMI Allowed = FOIR Capacity - Existing Monthly EMIs
3. Max Loan Principal = EMI × [ (1+r)^n - 1 ] / [ r × (1+r)^n ]
4. Overall Score = Credit (30%) + DTI (25%) + Income (20%) + LTI (15%) + Stability (10%)

Important Distinction

Calculated results represent educational decision support estimations based on standard mathematical formulas. Final loan approvals, interest rates, and sanction terms are governed exclusively by individual lending partners and formal credit underwriting committees.
